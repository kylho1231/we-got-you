# Security & Privacy Checklist (Philippines / RA 10173)

## 1. Statutory Compliance (Data Privacy Act of 2012)
- [x] **Data Minimization:** Only collect essential information needed for the dispatch or diagnostic appointment.
- [x] **Explicit Consent:** Positive consent required for location coordinates and device photos.
- [x] **Customer Coordinate Masking:** Public directory maps and listings never display personal home or breakdown coordinates. Only municipal / barangay proximity is displayed until an active job is accepted.
- [x] **Data Retention & Account Erasure:** Clear protocol allowing customers to request account closure and deletion of private profile logs.

## 2. Emergency & Consumer Safety Protocol
- [x] **Clear Separation from Official 911:** Persistent banner and disclaimers clarify that We Got You coordinates commercial repair and non-injury assistance.
- [x] **Life-Threatening Emergency Notice:** Direct shortcuts to dial 911, PNP Antique Provincial Police (0998 598 6236), and BFP San Jose Fire Station (0915 487 7509).
- [x] **Hazardous Diagnostics Warnings:** AI assistant and service pages explicitly instruct users never to touch high-voltage capacitors (microwaves), live electrical breaker panels, or leaking fuel lines.

## 3. Financial & Anti-Fraud Protection
- [x] **No Fake Verified Badges:** Badges are strictly segmented: Identity, Contact, Business Permit, and Technical Accreditation.
- [x] **No Fabricated Review Seeding:** Star ratings and reviews are locked strictly to customers with verified completed bookings.
- [x] **Prior Quotation Approval:** Technicians cannot start chargeable work without customer consent on itemized labor, parts, and travel fees.
- [x] **Direct Settlement Disclosures:** Cash upon completion and peer-to-peer GCash are clearly disclosed as independent direct settlements; no fraudulent escrow promises are made.
